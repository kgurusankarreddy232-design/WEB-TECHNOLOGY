🍬 Tirupati Laddu

Tirupati Laddu is the famous prasadam of Lord Venkateswara at Tirumala. It is prepared

mainly with gram flour, sugar, ghee, cashews, raisins and cardamom. The laddu is known

for its unique taste and traditional preparation. It is given to devotees after their

visit to the temple and has become an important symbol of the Tirumala pilgrimage.

🏛️ Sri Venkateswara Museum

Sri Venkateswara Museum is a famous cultural and heritage museum in Tirumala.

It displays objects, sculptures, photographs and information related to the history,

art and traditions of Tirumala. Visitors can learn about the development of the temple

and It is a peaceful and educational place for tourists, families and students.

The museum contains ancient sculptures, temple artifacts, photographs, paintings

and traditional objects. Visitors can learn about the architecture

festivals and historical development of Tirumala.
